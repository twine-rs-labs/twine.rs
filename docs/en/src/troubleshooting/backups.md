# Backups

The best way to prevent losing work in Twine is to back up your work regularly.

## In App Twine

App Twine makes a backup copy of your work when the application starts and on a
schedule while you are working. The default cadence is 20 minutes. By default,
it creates a folder called Backups next to the story library folder. That is, if
your story library folder is in Home › Documents › Twine › Stories, your
backups are in Home › Documents › Twine › Backups.

Use the _Backups_ panel in [Settings](../customizing/preferences.md) to change
the cadence and retention, run a backup immediately, record a backup review, or
reveal the backup folder. The location of the backup folder can also be set
[using a command-line switch](../customizing/command-line.md).

You can also keep backups of your own by copying your story library folder, or
individual stories, to another location.

## In Browser Twine

You must keep backups yourself using the
[Archive](../story-library/exporting.md) feature.

## What If I Forgot to Keep Backups And Thing Went Wrong?

You may not be completely out of luck.

In addition to automatic backups, app Twine creates files in the [scratch
folder](../publishing/scratch-folder.md). Files in this folder are created when you
play, test, or proof a story. You may be able to recover your work from files
here.
