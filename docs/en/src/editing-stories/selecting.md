# Selecting and Moving Passages

## Selecting Passages

With the graph's **Select** tool active, click a passage to select it.
Shift-click, Command-click on macOS, or Control-click on other platforms adds
or removes a passage from the selection. Drag from empty canvas to make a
marquee selection. Double-click a passage to open its editor.

Use **Select All** or **Deselect All** in the **Passage** action tab when needed.
See [graph navigation](navigating.md) for the Pan tool, context menus, zoom, and
keyboard controls.

## Moving Passages

Drag a selected node to move the selection. Movement follows the story's
**Snap to Grid** setting, available in **Story > Details**. Changing that
setting does not rearrange existing passage positions. Use project Undo to
reverse a move while its history is available.
