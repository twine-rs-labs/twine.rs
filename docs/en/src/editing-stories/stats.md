# Viewing Story Statistics

Choose **Story > Details** in the workbench. The panel shows character and word
counts, passage count, links and broken links, the last-change date, and the
story's IFID. Counts may show a placeholder while the current summary loads.

Counts describe the authoring sources, not the number of words a player sees
on a particular playthrough. The IFID identifies the story for interchange
and cataloging; see the [Interactive Fiction Database](https://ifdb.org/help-ifid).
